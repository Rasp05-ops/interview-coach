JOB TITLE: Software Engineer (Backend / Systems)
COMPANY: Apex Cloud Systems
LOCATION: New York, NY (Hybrid)
EMPLOYMENT TYPE: Full-time

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Apex Cloud Systems is seeking a Software Engineer to join our Core Backend 
Infrastructure team. In this role, you will design, build, and maintain high-throughput 
microservices and data processing systems that power our real-time analytics platform.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Design, code, and deploy robust, high-performance backend microservices using 
  Python and C++.
* Optimize data storage, caching layer performance, and database queries (SQL/NoSQL).
* Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code following industry best practices.
* Collaborate with product managers, frontend engineers, and DevOps teams to 
  deliver new system features.
* Monitor, debug, and troubleshoot production issues to maintain service reliability 
  and low latency.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENTS & QUALIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field 
  (or equivalent experience).
* 2+ years of professional software development experience in Python, C++, or Go.
* Demonstrated experience designing and consuming RESTful or gRPC APIs.
* Hands-on experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and caching 
  layers (Redis, Memcached).
* Familiarity with containerization tools (Docker) and Cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
* Strong understanding of Data Structures, Algorithms, and System Design principles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NICE-TO-HAVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Experience with distributed task queues, event streaming (Kafka/RabbitMQ), or 
  multi-threaded programming.
* Exposure to modern AI/ML frameworks or agentic workflow orchestration.
* Active contributions to open-source software projects.
